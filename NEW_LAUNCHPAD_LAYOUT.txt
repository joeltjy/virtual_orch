NEW LAUNCHPAD LAYOUT
====================

Written 21 Nov 2026 from the uncommitted work in ~/virtual_orch (branch main, files dated
19-20 Nov). Nothing here is committed or pushed yet. That work builds (build/ test binary of
20 Nov 13:15, newer than every changed file) and its tests pass: 73 of the 74
Launchpad / Accompaniment / PerformanceActivity / SeatingPlan / Pedal tests, the 74th
(SeatingPlanFreeze.writesSnapshotsWhenAskedTo) is skipped by design.

Part 1 describes the new layout and what else changed with it. Part 2 lists what still has to
be done, mainly to bring it together with the offline-sim branch (operation logs + log replay).


PART 1: THE NEW LAYOUT
----------------------

Rows are counted from the top (0) of the 8x8 grid, columns from the left (0). "side" = the
round button at the right end of a row (column 9 in the code). The top row of round buttons
(CC 104-111) is unchanged.

Old layout (main @ 9563268, what every recorded session used):

          col 0      1        2        3       4       5       6       7      | side
  top     Force rst  RT pause OT pause Block-in  -       -       -       -
  row 0   Edit       Jam      Accomp   -       -       -       -       -      |
  row 1   TD 1       TD 2     TD 4     TD 8    TD 16   TD 32   TD 64   -      | TD AUTO
  row 2   in:StrEns  in:Pizz  in:Trem  -       in:Fl   in:Ob   in:Cl   in:Bsn | FLUSH
  row 3   in:Horn    in:Tpt   in:Tbn   in:Tuba in:Timp -       -       -      |
  row 4   mdl:StrEns mdl:Pizz mdl:Trem -       mdl:Fl  mdl:Ob  mdl:Cl  mdl:Bsn|
  row 5   mdl:Horn   mdl:Tpt  mdl:Tbn  mdl:Tuba mdl:Timp -     -       -      |
  row 6   Reg LOW    Reg MED  Reg HIGH -       -       -       -       -      | Reg AUTO
  row 7   Poly 1     Poly 2   Poly 3   Poly 4  -       -       -       -      |

New layout:

          col 0      1        2        3      || 4       5        6        7      | side
  top     Force rst  RT pause OT pause Block-in  (unchanged)
  row 0   Step-in    Jam      Accomp   -      || -       -        -        -      |
  row 1   TD 1       TD 2     TD 4     TD 8   || TD 16   TD 32    TD 64    -      | TD AUTO
          ---------- YOUR instruments --------||---------- MODEL's instruments ---
  row 2   StrEns     Pizz     Tremolo  -      || StrEns  Pizz     Tremolo  -      | FLUSH (Step-in only)
  row 3   Flute      Oboe     Clarinet Bassoon|| Flute   Oboe     Clarinet Bassoon| RESET (Step-in only)
  row 4   Horn       Trumpet  Trombone Tuba   || Horn    Trumpet  Trombone Tuba   |
  row 5   Timpani    -        -        -      || Timpani -        -        -      |
  row 6   Reg LOW    Reg MED  Reg HIGH -      || Poly 1  Poly 2   Poly 3   Poly 4 | Reg AUTO
  row 7   Solemn  Mysterious Tender  Playful  || Passionate Heroic Stormy   Grand  |   (mood presets)

Summary of the move:
- Instruments: one row per family (strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion) instead of two
  rows for "input" and two rows for "model". Your side is columns 0-3, the model's is 4-7,
  with the same instrument in the same place on both sides (LaunchpadGrid::targetForPad,
  AppSession kInstrumentPads: rowOffset from kInstrumentRowFirst, col within the side).
- Max polyphony moved from row 7 cols 0-3 to row 6 cols 4-7 (kAccompRow == kRegisterRow,
  kPolyphonyColFirst = 4). Same behaviour: works and lit in Jam and Accompaniment, pressing
  the selected pad again clears the cap, entering Accompaniment with no cap starts at 1.
- Row 7 is new: eight mood presets.
- Row 3's side button is new: RESET.
- "Edit" is now called "Step-in" everywhere the user sees it (UI buttons, the MODE session-log
  line, PerformanceActivity "Mode: Step-in"). The code still says OrchestrationMode::Edit.

Which side the pads control (new):
- Step-in (Edit): your side is controlled; both sides respond to presses.
- Jam / Accompaniment: the model's side is controlled; presses on your side are ignored.
  (Old: input and model rows both worked in every mode.)
- Lighting (LaunchpadLighting.h): the controlled side is lit in family colours; the other side
  shows yours in grey (kGrey = 1) and the model's dimmed (dimmed(colour, kOtherSideDimLevel = 2),
  "tune on the hardware"). The on-screen grid (ActiveInstrumentsView) uses the same colours,
  draws a divider between the sides and a white frame around the controlled side, and its
  bracket label says "You" or "Model".

Flush and Reset (side buttons of rows 2 and 3):
- FLUSH (row 2): only in Step-in, and only lit there. Your instruments that are on are ADDED to
  the model's; yours stay on. (Old: worked in every mode, and your pads switched off after the
  flush.) Action text "Flush: your instruments added to the model".
- RESET (row 3, red): only in Step-in. Every model instrument switches off.
  Action text "Reset: model instruments cleared".

Mood presets (row 7; MoodPresets.h, a new file; edit its table to change them):

  pad  name        colour  instruments                                   TD    register
  0    Solemn      blue    StrEns Bassoon Horn Trombone Tuba             2     LOW
  1    Mysterious  purple  Tremolo Clarinet Bassoon                      2     LOW
  2    Tender      sky     StrEns Clarinet Horn                          4     AUTO
  3    Playful     lime    Pizz Flute Oboe Bassoon                       16    HIGH
  4    Passionate  pink    StrEns Oboe Clarinet Horn Trombone            16    AUTO
  5    Heroic      orange  StrEns Trumpet Horn Trombone Timpani          16    MEDIUM
  6    Stormy      red     Tremolo Trumpet Trombone Tuba Timpani         32    LOW
  7    Grand       white   all twelve                                    64    AUTO

- A press makes the CONTROLLED side's instruments exactly the preset's, then sets the
  register, then TD (TD last, so the register's "switch TD to AUTO" does not undo it).
- The app then cuts the future once from now + 1 s, as for a TD press (AppSession
  preset_callback, CUT line "preset <name>: notes from ... removed").
- Lights: the active preset full colour; dimmer once anything it sets was changed by hand
  (markPresetModified: TD, TD AUTO, register, register AUTO, a pad on the controlled side);
  the others dimmest. A mode change clears the active preset. Pressing it again re-applies it.
- Action text "Preset <name> (model)" / "(yours)".

Computer keyboard as Launchpad (KeyboardLaunchpad.h), new bindings:

  1-7 TD, 8 TD AUTO, 9 0 - register LOW/MED/HIGH, = register AUTO   (unchanged)
  Q W E         strings    StrEns Pizz Tremolo
  A S D F       woodwinds  Flute Oboe Clarinet Bassoon
  Z X C V       brass      Horn Trumpet Trombone Tuba
  T             timpani
                (instrument keys press the controlled side: yours in Step-in, the model's
                in Jam / Accompaniment, via KeyboardLaunchpad::resolve; Shift no longer used)
  Return        FLUSH            Backspace   RESET
  Y U I         mode Step-in / Jam / Accompaniment       (was Z X C)
  N M , .       max polyphony 1-4                         (was V B N M)
  F5 ... F12    mood presets 1-8 (new)
  F1 F2 F3 F4   force restart, RT pause, OT pause, block input   (unchanged)

The pedal (changed in the same work, not part of the grid but it goes with it):
- The app's pedal is now inverted. Pedal DOWN = the orchestra plays; pedal UP = it stops
  (generation paused, playback cleared, future cut from that moment). Old: pedal down paused
  and cut, pedal up resumed.
- Selecting a pedal device starts silenced (MidiInputProcess::pedalDeviceSelected, also when
  the saved device is restored at startup): the orchestra sounds once the pedal goes down.
  Clearing the pedal device un-silences (pedalDeviceCleared). With no pedal device nothing is
  paused until the first CC64.
- Renames: onPedalDown -> onPedalSilence, pedalDownSinceMs -> pedalSilentSinceMs,
  getPedalDownForMs -> getPedalSilentForMs; the seating-plan "frozen" display now follows
  pedal-up time. CUT line "pedal up: future removed from ...".

PART 2: CHANGES THAT HAVE TO BE MADE
------------------------------------

A. Commit the layout work
   1. Commit it on its own branch off main (e.g. launchpad-two-sided), leaving out
      cmake/CPM.cmake. OrchestrationModels.cpp can be left out too (same fix as f73a483).
   2. Decide on a version marker for the layout (e.g. LaunchpadGrid::kLayoutVersion = 2), so
      logs can say which layout a session used (see C3 and D).

B. Merge with offline-sim (operation logs, log replay; pushed, 56a4aaa)
   Files both sides changed, and what to do in each:
   - LaunchpadGrid.h: offline-sim adds a PAD_PRESS record (and OpLog::currentPad) at the top of
     handleInput and handleSceneInput. Layout-independent: keep it as is on top of the new code.
   - AppSession.cpp:
     * offline-sim wraps KeyboardLaunchpad::press in OpLog::padKey = label; keep that around
       the new press call (resolve() runs inside press, so PAD_PRESS logs the resolved pad).
     * offline-sim passes reasons to requestModeCut for TD / register / left Accompaniment;
       add one to the new preset_callback: "preset <name> pNN: next 1 s kept".
     * offline-sim's MODE record names modes Edit / Jam / Accompaniment; decide whether the
       oplog says "Step-in" (user-facing name) or keeps "Edit" (code name), and say so in the
       README. Suggestion: "Step-in", matching the session log.
     * PAD_ACTION is emitted from action_callback, so Reset and Preset actions are logged
       without further work.
   - MidiInputProcess.cpp (handleSustainPedal) - semantic, not just textual:
     * offline-sim logs PEDAL with state "down"/"up" and calls requestModeCut(pedalTime,
       "pedal down") and setGenerationPause(pause, "pedal"). In the new code the cut happens on
       pedal UP: the reason must become "pedal up", and the `stopped` edge replaces `pressed`.
     * Add oplog records for pedalDeviceSelected / pedalDeviceCleared (they pause / resume
       generation): e.g. PEDAL_DEVICE {selected: true}, and setGenerationPause(true,
       "pedal device selected") so RT_PAUSE has a reason.
     * PEDAL should say what the pedal did, not only the raw state: e.g. effect "stop" /
       "play", since "down" means the opposite in old and new sessions.
   - OrchestrationModels.cpp: identical fix on both sides; take either.
   - After the merge: build, run the whole test suite, and the LogReplay tests (see D, which
     will fail until D is done).

C. Operation-log format (orchestration/performance_streams/oplogs/README.md)
   1. PAD_PRESS row/col now refer to the new layout; document both layouts (or link this file).
   2. PEDAL: document the new polarity and the PEDAL_DEVICE record.
   3. SESSION_START: add `launchpad_layout` (1 = old, 2 = new) so a reader can interpret
      PAD_PRESS without knowing the date.
   4. New PAD_ACTION texts: "Reset: model instruments cleared", "Preset <name> (model|yours)",
      "Flush: your instruments added to the model", "Mode: Step-in".

D. Log replay of the OLD sessions (LogReplay.cpp, LogReplayTest, the oplog fill-in)
   Every recorded session (Oct-Nov 2026) used the old layout and the old pedal. After the merge
   the replay feeds their raw Launchpad notes and CC64 values to the new code, which would read
   them wrongly (an old "model: Str Ens" press at row 4 col 0 is now "your Horn"; an old row-7
   polyphony press is now a preset; the pedal is inverted). The permanent LogReplay test would
   fail. Needed:
   1. A legacy mode for replays of old sessions (Options::legacyLayout, set automatically for
      sessions before the change):
      - translate old pad presses to the new pad with the same meaning (old rows 2-3 -> your
        side, rows 4-5 -> model side, using the family rows; old row 7 cols 0-3 -> row 6 cols
        4-7; modes, TD, register unchanged);
      - invert the app pedal's CC64 (value v -> 127 - v; this maps v >= 64 onto < 64 exactly),
        and do not call pedalDeviceSelected (old sessions started un-silenced);
      - old FLUSH: worked in every mode and switched your pads off afterwards. Replay it as
        the new flush plus switching your pads off, and allow it outside Step-in;
      - old Jam / Accompaniment allowed presses on the input rows; the new grid ignores
        presses on your side outside Step-in. The replay must apply them anyway (e.g. set the
        user-side pad state directly), or old sessions that changed input instruments in Jam
        will diverge.
   2. The replay compares log TEXT with the recorded log: the new code writes "Step-in"
      (MODE line, "Mode: Step-in"), "Flush: your instruments added to the model" and
      "pedal up: future removed". In legacy mode either write the old texts or map them
      before comparing (LogReplay compareTag), so MODE / LAUNCHPAD / CUT stay exact.
   3. Re-run the LogReplay tests (all must pass as before), then the oplog checks
      (oplog_check.py) before regenerating the oplogs of the old sessions.

E. Performance streams (orchestration/tools/performance_streams)
   1. build_streams.py launchpad_button_name() hard-codes the old layout (_PAD_INSTR, rows
      2-5 input/model, row 7 polyphony, "Mode: Edit", side 2 = flush). Choose the layout by
      session date (or by the layout marker once logs carry it) and add the new one: family
      rows with your/model sides, row 6 cols 4-7 polyphony, row 7 presets, side 3 = Reset.
   2. Pedal parsing: it treats CC64 >= 64 on the app's pedal device as "down" = stopping
      generation. For new sessions the meaning is inverted (down = playing); the Controls
      track should show when the orchestra was stopped, not only the raw pedal position.
   3. Keyboard-Launchpad lines ("KEYBOARD_LAUNCHPAD key Q | pad 4 0") change meaning: new
      sessions log the resolved pad, so the key alone no longer says which side.
   4. Viewer labels: "Mode: Step-in", "Reset", the preset names.

F. To check on the hardware / decide
   - Dim level of the model's side (kOtherSideDimLevel = 2) and the grey of yours: readable on
     the Mini MK3 in a lit room?
   - The preset table (instruments, TD, register) is a first guess; tune it by playing.
   - F5-F12 on a Mac keyboard need fn unless "Use F1, F2, etc. keys as standard function keys"
     is on.
   - Row 5 has only Timpani on each side; cols 1-3 and 5-7 of row 5 are free (snare was dropped
     because its channel is the contrabass now).
   - Scripted MIDI input files (scripted-input work, a2240be) that contain Launchpad notes were
     written for the old layout and must be updated.
